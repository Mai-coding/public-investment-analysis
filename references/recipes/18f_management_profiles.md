# Recipe 18f — Management Profiles and Credibility (§9)

## 1. Management summary card

Create a 2×3 card grid for 5–6 key executives: initials/photo, name, role/tenure and two short achievements. It is navigation only and cannot replace the prose profiles.

## 2. Numbered deep profiles — hard requirement

Write 5–7 separate H3 blocks under §9.3 using headings such as:

`9.3.1 Jane Doe｜CEO`  
`9.3.2 John Doe｜CFO`

Each block contains:

- background, join date and decision scope;
- 2–3 quantified track-record facts relevant to the current thesis;
- operating/capital-allocation style supported by examples;
- specific responsibility for the next 2–3 year value-creation plan;
- one risk, controversy, dependency or unproven capability.

Target 180–300 Chinese characters per profile. A role list or two-line bio is not a profile. `report_content_audit.py` checks at least five `9.x.x` blocks, numerical evidence and a risk signal.

## 3. Organization and governance

After the profiles, analyze responsibility boundaries, succession, board oversight, incentive design, SBC/insider alignment, key-person risk and acquired-team retention. Connect each issue to a measurable KPI.

## 4. Guidance credibility

Place the latest five-call guidance evolution in §9, not §3. Separate short-cycle quarterly guidance from long-term targets, explain misses/beats, and map credibility into forecast ranges and valuation confidence.

## 5. Photos

Priority: user-provided → company leadership page → Wikimedia with license. If unavailable, use initials; do not spend time searching SEC proxy files for portraits. Captions use numeric source markers under `20_citation_and_source_appendix.md`.
